## Review Focus

1. Restored/forged/missing state must not invent evidence, repeated awards, or a verdict: Task 5 owns malformed-state tests.
2. A screen left open at action six must follow the same rules as navigation back into it: Task 2 owns cap/UI integration tests.
3. Input queued after the deadline or while a tab was hidden cannot produce a late human win: Task 3 owns exact-deadline and resume tests.
4. Notebook notes, unowned facts, and future scene content cannot enter proof or telemetry: Tasks 2/5 own injection and exclusion tests.
5. A successful local URL round-trip cannot close a failed or unknown external survey return: Task 6 owns the external-evidence gate and historical-return review.
