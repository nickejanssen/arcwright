# AW-315: Study The Fourteen Inspirations Against Specific Design Decisions

**Milestone / Epic:** M6 / M6-D  
**Size:** S  
**Product:** Shared (Arcwright and Nightcap)  
**Owner:** One design session with the founder  
**Target date:** 2027-01-30

## Plain-English Summary

The founder named fourteen games and books that inspire him and said the first mapping was accurate but simplistic. Study each for mechanics, story handling, artistry, expandability and immersion, tied to a decision Nightcap or Arcwright has to make.

## Why This Matters

The inspirations are recorded in `docs/product/2026-11-15-reset-audit.md` with one lesson each. The founder values each for much more than one lesson.

## Technical Scope

- Murdle, Jackbox, Dungeon Crawler Carl, Skyrim, No Man's Sky, Mario Party, It Takes Two, Split Fiction, Pokemon, RollerCoaster Tycoon, Civilization V, Roblox, Unravel 2, Minecraft.
- For each: what it does, the evidence (maker statements where they exist), and one decision it informs.
- Label each claim verified or inferred.

**Interaction profile:** Creative collaboration.
