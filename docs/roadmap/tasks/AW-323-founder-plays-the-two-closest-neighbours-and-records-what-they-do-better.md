# AW-323: Founder Plays The Two Closest Neighbours And Records What They Do Better

**Milestone / Epic:** M6 / M6-D  
**Size:** S  
**Product:** Arcwright  
**Owner:** The founder, about 40 minutes; an AI session writes up his notes  
**Target date:** 2026-12-20

## Plain-English Summary

The competitor comparison of 15 November 2026 was done by reading, not by playing. Two products sit closest to what Arcwright wants to be. The founder plays each for about twenty minutes and says what they do better, what they do worse, and whether anything changes the plan.

## Why This Matters

- Hidden Door now sells something close to "a dungeon master for any world": a studio for modelling a world, and a way for other products to run stories in it. The reset audit had said nobody did this.
- TableForge says its rules engine, not the AI, owns outcomes. That is one of Arcwright's four claimed differences.
- Reading a maker's own page shows what they claim. Only playing shows whether it is any good.
- Source: `docs/product/2026-11-15-competitor-and-prior-art.md`. Captured under D-119.

## Technical Scope

- Play one Hidden Door world and one TableForge session, about twenty minutes each, on their free tiers.
- Record, in the founder's own words: what felt good, what felt bad, and one thing Nightcap should take or avoid.
- An AI session adds the notes to the comparison document and says which of the four claimed differences change.

## Human Collaboration Contract

**Interaction profile:** Facilitated live operation, then a decision interview.

- Founder inputs: forty minutes of play and spoken or typed notes.
- Any account is created by the founder himself. No AI session signs up for anything.
- Approval: the founder says whether anything changes the plan; the answer is recorded on the issue.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The founder has played both and his notes are in the comparison document.
- [ ] Each of the four claimed differences is marked unchanged or changed, with a reason.
- [ ] Any change to the plan is recorded as a decision or a new task.

## Tests/Verification

- Founder review of the written notes.

## Dependencies

- AW-316 (done 2026-11-15).

## Must Not Do

- Do not let this delay the first case or session 1. It can wait until after session 1.
- Spend nothing. Stay on free tiers.
- Do not copy another product's text, art or worlds.
