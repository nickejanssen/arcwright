# Nightcap — Espionage & Leverage

**Status:** Foundational direction DECIDED; implementation details TESTING  
**Type:** GDD system one-pager

## Purpose

Espionage manipulates the **information race around the murder**.

It should change what players investigate, verify, conceal, reveal, bluff about, protect, or race toward rather than become a separate PvP damage economy.

## Rival Presence

Nightcap's competition should be legible throughout the investigation without exposing private theory state.

Players should regularly understand that rival detectives are independently investigating and changing the competitive landscape. The game may reveal facts such as that another detective:

- questioned a suspect;
- pursued a location/object/lead;
- reached an opportunity first;
- earned or spent a competitive resource;
- triggered a shared event; or
- changed interaction order, access, or visibility.

This should create urgency and rivalry without turning the experience into constant status spam.

Rivals' private notebooks, private evidence where applicable, and final theories remain private unless deliberately shared.

A solo authored rival used in a test fixture is **research scaffolding only**. It does not define canonical multiplayer behavior and should never be treated as evidence that real player rivalry is validated.

## Core Laws

- Espionage-first Leverage fantasy.
- Heavy information warfare.
- Mostly additive interaction.
- Observe/copy rather than destroy.
- **Knowledge ≠ Proof.**
- Finite Leverage with competing uses so "always spy" is not automatically dominant.
- Small capped Leverage bank.
- A player can gain ordinary investigative leverage simply by discovering, noticing, or understanding useful information first; this does not automatically become a spendable Leverage point.
- Minigames may grant Leverage or other temporary advantages, but repeated minigame wins must not become the primary gateway to espionage or compound into control of the investigation. Exact Leverage sourcing is **TESTING**.
- Keep the player-facing resource burden small; Leverage should not become a bookkeeping game.
- Counterintel exists.
- Nightcap does not grant automatic protection merely because several opponents target the same investigator. Concentrated targeting is legitimate strategy unless a repeatable mechanic materially prevents the target from continuing to play the investigation.
- **Big swings, short half-life.** Temporary access, priority, protection, or similar privileges should resolve or expire quickly; legitimate information learned through them remains learned.

## Leverage Value

Leverage is primarily **information + opportunity**.

It may:

- expose what another investigator pursued;
- reveal limited competitive intelligence;
- create a bounded opportunity to act on that intelligence;
- affect access, protection, priority, exposure, or disclosure;
- alter timing or interaction order.

It may inconvenience, delay, or redirect a rival, but must not:

- change canonical truth;
- erase legitimately learned facts;
- permanently delete required evidence;
- create unfalsifiable evidence;
- let a model decide whether sabotage succeeded; or
- remove every legitimate route to an essential truth.

Espionage should create another murder-facing decision rather than merely inflict damage.

## Availability

Leverage is player-controlled, but its strongest uses are **contextual**.

The evolving case creates meaningful opportunities when another player acquires private information, an important route becomes contested, information may become public, a player exposes a vulnerability, or a valuable opportunity enters play.

The player decides whether the moment deserves the finite resource.

## Social Visibility

Espionage uses **uncertain attribution** where the player count and fiction allow it.

Players may know that information warfare occurred without always knowing who caused it, exactly what was learned, or what the actor intends next.

At two players, obvious attribution is accepted where unavoidable. Uncertainty shifts toward **what was learned, copied, protected, or intended** rather than pretending the actor is unknowable.

## Fair Deception

Nightcap permits fair-play mystery deception, including:

- red herrings;
- lying or mistaken witnesses;
- forged documents;
- staged scenes;
- planted objects;
- manipulated recordings;
- misleading context;
- deliberate omissions;
- other literary or cinematic devices.

> **The fiction may lie. The game must play fair.**

Deception must remain consistent with the authored truth and reasonably interrogable through the case. False information should exist because something or someone in the fictional world produced it, not because the system secretly changes reality to punish a player.

## Counterintel Boundary

Primarily detect, expose, block, trace, or misdirect. It protects advantage rather than becoming another evidence-production engine.

## Solvability

Competitive manipulation may delay, obscure, complicate, redirect, or temporarily disadvantage investigation.

It must not make the canonical mystery unfairly unsolvable. Essential truths retain fair alternate routes.

## Handoff

**RIVAL INFORMATION / OPPORTUNITY → LEVERAGE DECISION → INFORMATION OR TACTICAL CHANGE → REACT → INVESTIGATE / VERIFY / BLUFF / PROTECT**

## TESTING

- exact Leverage costs and frequency;
- Heat behavior;
- Counterintel strength;
- repeated targeting;
- how much rival activity should be surfaced without becoming noisy;
- how rival visibility scales from two players to larger rooms;
- **PARKED:** rare auctions; reopen only if a concrete case or gameplay problem gives them a clear purpose;
- repeated-targeting balance and whether any chainable action can suppress investigation participation; do not add pity immunity or target-rotation rules without evidence.
