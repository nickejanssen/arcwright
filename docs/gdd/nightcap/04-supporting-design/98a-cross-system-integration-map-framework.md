**Status:** ADOPTED supporting design-control artifact — not game canon

## Purpose

Ensure Nightcap's systems behave like one murder-mystery game rather than individually optimized modules.

The framework itself is adopted as a working method. Canon still lives in the decision ledger and authoritative system one-pagers.

## Core Question

> **Why does this system happen here, what does it change, and why am I thinking about the murder differently afterward?**

## Cross-System Laws Established in the Cohesion Pass

- Strong invisible authorship; high perceived player autonomy.
- Investigation may reward information alone; the player owns implication.
- THINK is continuous with selected intentional actions, not recurring homework.
- Player hypotheses may feed bounded verification/challenge actions.
- Major minigames must reconnect to murder-facing state.
- Minigames are opportunity-first rather than proof-first.
- Espionage manipulates the information race, not generic PvP health.
- **The fiction may lie. The game must play fair.**
- REACT is naturalistic but legible and may create real tactical consequences.
- Re-entry exposes **state, not inference**.
- Two-player Nightcap preserves the full core loop with tighter social geometry.
- Last Call uses authored dramatic timing constrained by fair solvability.
- V1 endgame uses deterministic Structured Reconstruction and simultaneous private lock.
- Nightcap tracks legitimate player knowledge/ownership automatically without telling players which facts matter or which reconstruction is correct.
- After lock, only accused culprits are revealed before The Truth; players do not present cases to the room.
- The hidden authored case graph supports multiple valid routes, deterministic solve checking, and Arcwright authoring/complexity analytics.
- The endgame must feel like payoff, not essay-writing or a lecture.
- Truth → Verdict → Postmortem own narrative → competitive → social catharsis.

## Remaining Cohesion Risks

These are now primarily execution / validation risks rather than ownerless architecture:

- autonomy vs. action clarity,
- diegetic investigation UX,
- Case Board usefulness vs. administration,
- minigame-to-murder transition quality,
- espionage balance and dogpiling,
- two-player quality,
- mixed-skill competitive fairness,
- endgame pacing,
- upper player-count viability,
- social-play balance across bluffing, information warfare, player-count geometry, and table dominance.

## Next Use

Use this map during future adversarial passes and prototype interpretation. Do not add new cohesion laws merely to make the matrix more complete; reopen architecture only when a real contradiction, missing owner, or evidence-supported problem appears.
