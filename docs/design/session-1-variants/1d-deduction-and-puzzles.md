# Version 1d: Deduction And Puzzles

Part of [the version overview](README.md). Proposed, not approved. Founder's words (D-146): "a maximize deduction, investigation, and puzzles version".

## Who it is for

Players who like working things out: the *Murdle* and escape-room crowd. Played only if a fourth group is found.

## What players do and see (invented example)

Every mini-game is framed as evidence work when that is natural, and openly a game when it is not (D-143). Round 1: Search and Find as "scanning the scene for what does not belong". Round 2: a group Lock Picking on a cash box that everyone has access to; first to open wins the time to read what is inside. Round 3: Battle of Wits with logic and mental-math questions only ("Three suspects, one lies: who?"), no trivia. The host reminds players what they have found, never what it means. The winner of each game gets one extra investigation (D-126, spec 0094), never case information.

## Mini-games (a starting setup, not a rule; D-145)

Search and Find (control), Lock Picking, Battle of Wits in logic-only form. `fitting`, no repeat. Solo Lock Picking may also appear if AW-345 is built; solo runs do not count (founder answer 9). Investigations stay at six per player (spec 0092 Q5, a deliberate choice).

## What it tests

- Do puzzle-style mini-games make players feel they worked it out themselves?
- Is logic-only Battle of Wits better liked than trivia for this cohort?
- Does a lock to pick feel like detective work?

## What the survey adds

The shared questions, plus: "Did you feel you worked it out yourself? (1 to 5)" and "Which puzzle was the best one?"

## What it costs

| Part | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Arc setup | Arc file only |
| Lock Picking | New mechanic (shared with 1c). The group form needs everyone to receive the same lock at once |
| Logic-only question bank | Writing |
| Deeper puzzle mechanics (comparing samples, rebuilding a damaged record, tracing a timeline; GDD Story Fit Rule) | Not in December. New mechanics after the first group sessions |

## Ready if (15 December)

Lock Picking built, a logic question set written, and a fourth group found.

## Prior art

*Murdle*: solo, daily, pure deduction (reset audit, section 3). *Unlock!* and *Exit: The Game*: escape-room card games where puzzles gate progress and difficulty is rated (recalled). *Return of the Obra Dinn*: deducing identities from evidence with no hand-holding (recalled).
