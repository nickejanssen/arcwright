# Version 1b: Newcomers

Part of [the version overview](README.md). Proposed, not approved. The founder chose this as the fourth version ("Newcomers instead", answer 1), over a bluffing version.

## Who it is for

Friends who rarely play games and may not know what a "round" or a "mini-game" is. This is the version that tests the thing session 3 depends on: can people play it with nobody coaching them?

## What players do and see (invented example)

The host says at the start of every phase what to do in plain words: "Tap one place to look into. You have six looks in the whole game." Round 1 has a Search and Find: everyone gets the same picture of a hotel lobby on their phone, and the host says, "Find the green umbrella. Tap it when you see it." There is no clock bar until the second time a phase appears. Round 2 has no mini-game, only a scene and investigating. In round 3 there is one Battle of Wits with easy questions ("Which colour do you get by mixing blue and yellow?"). No final race. Last Call is explained the same way: "Pick who, how and why from what you found. Nobody sees it until the end."

## Mini-games (a starting setup, not a rule; D-145)

Two mini-games in three rounds: Search and Find (control) in round 1, none in round 2, Battle of Wits (easy bank) in round 3. `mini_game_pick: fitting`, so no mechanic repeats (D-136, D-147). No solo games. A longer phase clock where the pacing setting allows it (arc file).

## What it tests

- Can a first-time player follow the game without asking the host? (The survey counts host questions in every version, so this one has a baseline.)
- Does a lighter setup still feel like being a detective?
- Is it fun for people who do not like racing?

## What the survey adds

The shared questions, plus: "Could you follow what to do without help? (yes, mostly, no)". Also counted: how many times someone asked the host a question.

## What it costs

| Part | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Arc setup | Arc file only (verified: slots, `fitting`, zero slots allowed per AW-344 and D-147) |
| Mechanics | Search and Find and Battle of Wits, both shared with 1a |
| Extra host explanation | Writing: a plain-words line for each phase kind. Host lines are written by a person (spec 0094) |
| Easy question bank | Writing, a small set |

Cheapest of the four: it reuses the mechanics of 1a and mostly removes things.

## Ready if (5 December)

1a is built (1b adds nothing new to build), the easy question set and the extra host lines are written, and the version name is saved in the play record.

## Prior art

Jackbox: phones as the only controller, and a host that explains each round, is why non-gamers join (recalled, not checked). *Exit: The Game* and *Unlock!* print a difficulty rating on each scenario so a group can pick an easier one (recalled). Inference: a lower-difficulty version helps only if the host's wording, not the rules, carries the teaching; this version tests that.
