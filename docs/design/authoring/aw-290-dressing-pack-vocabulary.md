**Status:** Approved

## Purpose

This is newly authored wrapper dressing, not vocabulary extracted from the
moodboards. The moodboards provide tone and material reference but do not
contain a complete drink pool or the final Big Top stage-title vocabulary.

The values are aesthetic-only. They must not introduce plot facts, character
relationships, or gameplay behavior.
